Spheres and ellipsoids: a textured celestial body, as OpenFrames' Sphere.

The body is centered on its frame's origin (or at set_sphere_position, turned by set_sphere_attitude, e.g. to orient a texture). Its shape is a sphere of radius, or an ellipsoid with semi-axes radius * sphere_scale; the ellipsoid is built directly (exact normals, geodetic texture mapping; see vkf_geometry), where OpenFrames scales a sphere. The texture is an equirectangular image (longitude -180 to 180, north at the top). The color is used when there is no texture.

Lighting: the body is lit by the scene's lights (a frame's light source, e.g. the Sun, or the window's light), with a material (set_material; OpenFrames' default: no specular reflection). The night texture shows where the surface is lit weakly (the night side: city lights), as OpenFrames' setNightTextureMap: weighted by 1 - the lit color (the material's ambient and diffuse colors in the lights' colors and intensities, and its emission), per channel and per pixel, so a brighter light narrows the band where the day fades into the night, and ambient light dims the night texture. The twilight (set_twilight; not in OpenFrames; default none: the light ends at the terminator) extends the diffuse light past the terminator, to where the light is a given angle below the horizon, fading out: e.g. sunlight scattered by an atmosphere.

Translucency (not in OpenFrames): set_opacity (default 1, opaque) below 1 draws the body translucent: lit as usual, blended over what is behind it, drawn after the opaque scene (farthest first: VSG's depth-sorted bin) without writing depth, front faces only. Whether it is translucent is decided when a window first shows it (as the textures); its opacity can then change at any time (through the material's alpha).

Texture files are found with find_data_file (a path, or a name on VKFRAMES_DATA_PATH, e.g. data/'s 2k_earth_daymap.jpg and 2k_earth_nightmap.jpg). The textures and the mesh resolution are read when a window first shows the sphere: set them before that. The radius, scale, color and material can change at any time: the meshes are built once, then scaled by their transform (radii / the built radii), and the color is applied through the material (the shaders multiply the vertex color, white here, by the diffuse color).

Level of detail (set_auto_lod, on by default; OpenFrames' setAutoLOD): the body has meshes of 32, 64, ... 1024 segments around (half as many from pole to pole), and each view draws the coarsest whose facets stay within half a pixel of the true outline (src/vsg/vkf_layer.cpp: SphereLod), so that the body isn't faceted however close the view. Meshes are built when a view first wants them (the 128-segment one at once), in the sphere's update. Off: one mesh of set_resolution's segments (default 128 x 64).

private subroutine set_material(self, ambient, diffuse, specular, emission, shininess)

The material (OpenFrames' setMaterial; each optional): ambient, diffuse, specular and emission colors ([r, g, b] or [r, g, b, a], 0 to 1) and shininess (0 to 128: the specular highlight's sharpness; 0 for none). The ambient and diffuse colors scale the body's color or texture (VSG's Phong); the specular color is reflected from the lights' specular parts. Defaults: ambient and diffuse white, no specular reflection or emission.
