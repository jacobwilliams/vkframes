Diagnostics (not in OpenFrames): what vkframes is built with and runs on, and how fast a window draws, for an information or debugging panel.

  • library_versions(): the versions of vkframes, VSG, vsgXchange, Dear ImGui, the Vulkan headers it was built with and the Vulkan loader it runs with (LibraryVersions).
  • A window's device_info(): its Vulkan device and driver (DeviceInfo).
  • A window's render_stats(): its frame rate and where a frame's time goes (RenderStats), measured at every frame at no cost: the program's part (the scene's update and the event handler), and the renderer's: waiting for the next image to draw into (with vsync, this is mostly waiting for the display), recording and submitting, and presenting; the GPU's time for the frame (timestamps around its commands, read frames later, so it lags a few frames; not measured on devices without timestamps); and the device's memory in use and its budget (if the driver says).
  • A window's scene_counts(): the size of its scene graph (SceneCounts), counted when asked (a traversal of the whole graph: for an occasional update, not every frame).
