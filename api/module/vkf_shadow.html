Shadow cones: the regions of a celestial body's shadow in the light of another (e.g. the Earth's or the Moon's shadow in sunlight), drawn as transparent cones:

  • the umbra (total eclipse: the light hidden), converging behind the body to its apex, at d R / (Rs - R) from the body's center;
  • the antumbra (annular eclipse: the light seen around the body), from the umbra's apex outward, widening at the umbra's angle;
  • the penumbra (partial eclipse), widening behind the body.

(R and Rs: the body's and the light's radii; d: the distance between their centers. The edges are the lines tangent to both spheres: the outer tangents for the umbra and antumbra, half-angle asin((Rs - R) / d), the inner ones for the penumbra, asin((Rs + R) / d).) A body larger than its light has no apex: its umbra widens (or is a cylinder), with no antumbra.

A ShadowCones is a frame of its own, made a child of the body (add_child) for one body's shadow, and shown or hidden with show_contents: a scene without one does no shadow work at all. It refers to the body and the light frames (they must stay alive while set); each scene update, after the whole tree has moved (late_update), it moves itself to the body's center and turns its +z axis away from the light (its own position and attitude are set there, whatever its parent), and reshapes the cones for the distance between them: the cones' vertices are rewritten in place and uploaded only when the shape changed (not as the body only turns). Hidden, it does nothing (and is placed again when shown).

The radii: given (set_body_radius, set_light_radius), or the frames' own: a Sphere's equatorial radius, a TerrainBody's (a light that is a plain ReferenceFrame needs its radius given: none, no shadow). The shadow is drawn to set_length behind the body's center (default: twice the umbra's length; a hundred body radii without an apex); the umbra stops there if shorter. Each region has a color ([r, g, b, a]; the surfaces are unlit, transparent, seen from both sides, as the cones of vkf_shapes) and can be drawn or not (set_regions).

Bodies in a shadow are not darkened (that needs shadows in the lighting).
