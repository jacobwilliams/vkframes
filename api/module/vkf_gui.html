GUI panels in a window (not in OpenFrames): Dear ImGui panels, buttons, checkboxes and sliders, drawn flat on top of the scene, through vsgImGui.

A window's Gui (WindowProxy%gui(), made on first use) is described immediate-mode, each frame (e.g. in the event handler's before_draw, or in the frame loop before frame()): panels (begin_panel ... end_panel) holding text, buttons, checkboxes, sliders, combo boxes (drop-down menus), separators. A widget's call gives what the user did with it at the frame before: button is true once after a click; checkbox, slider and combo change the value given (and are true then). What isn't described in a frame isn't shown in it.

A panel is placed where ImGui puts it (and can be moved), or at a position (0 to 1 across the window, y up, as HUD text), at first or every frame (follow): e.g. next to a picked object, kept at its screen position (WindowProxy%screen_position). A title starting with '##' gives no title bar (a callout). Clicks, drags and keys over a panel go to it: the camera, the event handler and picking don't get them. The GUI is scaled by the window's pixel ratio. A Gui is a handle (copies are the same GUI), valid while its window is open.

private subroutine begin_panel(self, title, position, pivot, follow)

A panel titled title ('##name': no title bar), with the widgets up to end_panel. position ([x, y], 0 to 1 across the window, y up; default: where ImGui puts it) is where its point pivot is ([0, 0], the default: its top left; [0.5, 1]: the middle of its bottom); with follow, every frame (and it can't be moved), else at first.
