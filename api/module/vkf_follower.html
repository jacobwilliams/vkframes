Following trajectories: the position and attitude of a frame from one or more trajectories at the current time, as OpenFrames' TrajectoryFollower.

With several trajectories, the one followed at time t is the one whose time range contains t, or the closest one. If t is outside all of them, FOLLOW_LOOP wraps it into their combined time range and FOLLOW_LIMIT holds the ends. Positions are interpolated linearly, attitudes by slerp.

The time is either the global simulation time plus an offset (set_offset_time, the default with offset 0) or a fixed time (set_time). The trajectories are not owned: they must stay alive while followed.
