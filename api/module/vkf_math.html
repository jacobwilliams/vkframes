Math for vkframes: the working precision, quaternions and 4x4 transforms.

Quaternions are q(4) = [x, y, z, w] (vector part first, as OpenFrames and OpenSceneGraph store them), unit length for rotations. The rotation of q takes vectors in a frame to its parent frame: v_parent = rotate(q, v_local). Matrices are 4x4, column-major, acting on column vectors.
