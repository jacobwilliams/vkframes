Maps and terrain: a celestial body drawn from map layers by Rocky (https://github.com/pelicanmapping/rocky; optional: rocky_available()), e.g. the Earth from tile services, or the Moon with NASA's imagery and LOLA's elevation. An addition to OpenFrames, which has only textured spheres.

The body is centered on its frame's origin, in body-fixed axes (z through the north pole, x through longitude 0), so the frame's attitude turns it. Its shape and coordinates come from Rocky's tiling profile: '' (Rocky's default) or 'global-geodetic' for the Earth (WGS84), 'moon' for the Moon (a sphere of 1737.4 km), or another of Rocky's profiles. Rocky works in meters; the body is drawn at units_per_meter scene units per meter (default 1e-3: kilometers, as vkframes' examples).

Layers (add_image_layer, add_elevation_layer) are tiled sources Rocky pages in as the view needs them: a TMS service (LAYER_TMS), a file or service GDAL reads (LAYER_GDAL; a file is found with find_data_file, e.g. data/'s moon_lola_ldem16.tif from pixi run -e rocky moon-data), or a {z}/{x}/{y} URL template of geodetic tiles on the body (LAYER_GEODETIC: 2 x 1 tiles at level 0, rows from the top, as WMTS and NASA's Moon Trek) or of web Mercator tiles (LAYER_MERCATOR: OpenStreetMap-style). Elevation is in meters (ELEVATION_HEIGHTS) or encoded in RGB (ELEVATION_MAPBOX, ELEVATION_TERRARIUM). Layers can be added before or after the body is shown.

Lighting: lit by the scene's lights as a Sphere (a frame's light source, e.g. the Sun, or the window's light; set_lighting(.false.) shows the imagery as it is). The frame's color is the color where no imagery covers the body.

The map is made for a window that shows the body (Rocky's tiles are loaded through that window's viewer, and the map is drawn in that window only); when that window closes, it is made again for one still open (other windows then show it). Headless (no viewer), the body has no map.
