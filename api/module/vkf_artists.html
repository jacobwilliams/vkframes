Trajectory artists: what draws a Trajectory, in a DrawableTrajectory frame, as OpenFrames' TrajectoryArtist, CurveArtist, SegmentArtist, MarkerArtist and DrawableTrajectory, and RibbonArtist (not in OpenFrames: a translucent surface swept between two points at each trajectory point, e.g. an orbit's plane from the origin to the positions).

A DrawableTrajectory is a frame (its points are in its coordinates) holding artists. Each frame update, an artist draws what changed in its trajectory: appended points are written into the GPU buffers in place, and only the buffers written to are uploaded (see vkf_line_chain); a cleared trajectory is redrawn into the same buffers. The last point drawn is always rewritten, since set_optional changes it.

Trace mode (set_trace_mode) draws only the part of the trajectory up to the current time: the points whose times are at most the current time, whatever order they were added in (a change of the draw ranges, no upload): for increasing times its first points, for decreasing ones (a trajectory propagated backwards) its last. A curve adds a two-point tail from the latest of them to the point interpolated at the current time (the only data uploaded each frame). So a trajectory propagated forwards and one propagated backwards to the same state trace one history as time runs (example/trace_directions.f90); with time running backwards, the history shrinks back. (A highlight's arrows still run in the order the points were added: the propagation's direction.)

Point colors (not in OpenFrames): a CurveArtist draws a trajectory with colors (Trajectory%add_color, or add's color) in them, each segment blended from its first point's color to its second's (one color: that color); without colors, or with set_point_colors(.false.), in its own color. Its line buffers hold colors only then.

Trails (not in OpenFrames): CurveArtist%set_trail(past, future, fade) draws only the part of the curve within past before the current time and future after it, each fading out over the last fade of its window toward its cutoff; set_trail_style(TRAIL_PAST or TRAIL_FUTURE, ...) gives each part its own color (or translucency) and dash pattern (e.g. a solid past and a dashed future). The GPU fades and cuts the line by its distance along it (shaders/line.vert): each frame only the distances at the current time and at the window's edges are uploaded (a few floats per chunk), and the range of points drawn changed. It replaces trace mode while set.

Highlight (not in OpenFrames; e.g. a trajectory selected by the program or by picking): set_highlight on a curve or segment artist, or on a DrawableTrajectory for all of them. HIGHLIGHT_PULSE: the line widens and brightens towards the highlight color, and back, over and over; HIGHLIGHT_ARROWS: a translucent band of the highlight color around it, with chevrons moving along it from its first point to its last (a trajectory's initial time to its final; a segment's start to its end). Both together: HIGHLIGHT_PULSE + HIGHLIGHT_ARROWS. The GPU animates them (shaders/ line.vert), so nothing is uploaded or computed each frame. In trace mode, only the part drawn is highlighted.

Artists and their trajectories are not owned: they must stay alive while drawn.

Picking (WindowProxy%pick): a DrawableTrajectory picks what its artists draw, as drawn (in trace mode, up to the current time): a curve's segments (its chunks skipped by their bounds when far from the cursor), with the point and the time there (interpolated); a segment artist's segments; a marker artist's start, end and intermediate markers, with their times. Lines are reached within the tolerance plus half their width, markers plus half their size. Each artist gives its best hit: the closest to the cursor (and of those, the nearest).

type, private, abstract, extends(TrajectoryArtist) ::  ChainArtist

The base of the artists drawing a trajectory's points through one chain of buffers (CurveArtist: a line, a chain point per trajectory point; RibbonArtist: a surface, a pair of chain points per trajectory point): the chain and how much of it is written and drawn, the trace tail, the trajectory's colors, and the update (chain_update: the points written as they change, then drawn all or, in trace mode, those passed and the tail to the point at the current time). Each extension says what its chain is (chain_kind), how many points it draws (points), its tail's node (new_tail), its style (apply_style), how its points are written (write_points) and its tail drawn (draw_tail).
