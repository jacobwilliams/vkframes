Writing a scene for the web (WindowProxy%export_web): the page's scene description (JSON), its binary arrays (one buffer of float32, float64 and uint32 arrays, referred to from the JSON by byte offset and count), its assets (textures and models, copied from their files), and the page itself (one self-contained HTML file: the JSON, the arrays and the assets inlined, base64 for the binary ones, and vkframes' Three.js viewer, data/web/vkf_viewer.js).

Frames write what they draw through the item writers here (begin_line_item, begin_point_item, begin_mesh_item): positions in float32, cut into chunks that each have their own origin (in double precision), so that points far from the frame's origin keep their precision (the viewer places each chunk at its origin, and Three.js composes the transforms in double precision); times in float32 relative to the export's reference time (t_ref).
