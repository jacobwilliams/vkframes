public function ellipsoid_create(radii, rgba, texture, n_lon, n_lat, night_texture, translucent) result(mesh)

An ellipsoid mesh node with semi-axes radii (x, y, z) and color (rgba, multiplying the texture), with an optional equirectangular texture and night texture (image handles; the night texture shows where the light is weak). n_lon and n_lat default to 128 and 64 segments. With translucent, a translucent surface (its material's alpha; drawn after the opaque scene, sorted by depth). Null on failure.

public subroutine arrow(base, direction, body_length, head_length, body_radius, head_radius, n_segments, xyz, normals, uv, indices)

An arrow (OpenFrames' Vector: a cylinder body and a cone head) from base along direction: body of length body_length and radius body_radius, then a head of length head_length and base radius head_radius, closed at the bottom of the body and of the head, with n_segments around. Triangles are counter-clockwise seen from outside; indices are 0-based; uv is zero. Sizes are given by arrow_size.
