Reference shapes, as OpenFrames' frames of the same names: CoordinateAxes (axis lines with tick marks), LatLonGrid (latitude and longitude lines on an ellipsoid), RadialPlane (a transparent disk with circles and radial lines) and cones (PolyhedralCone, and its EllipticCone and RectangularCone: e.g. sensor fields of view); and (not in OpenFrames) Polyhedron, any surface of polygons or triangles, or a grid of points (e.g. a tube of trajectories), fixed in its frame, lit or not, opaque or translucent.

Each is a frame drawing in its own coordinates. Its drawing is made when a window first shows it and made again when a setting changes (also while shown; the new nodes are compiled and swapped in). Lines are vkframes' double-precision lines (widths in window units); tick marks are markers (MarkerArtist's shapes); surfaces are unlit and transparent, seen from both sides, and write no depth (as OpenFrames'), except a Polyhedron's when lit. Surfaces are made by one routine (surface_node), as their lines (line_node) and points (marker_node).

type, public, extends(ReferenceFrame) ::  CoordinateAxes

Coordinate axes with tick marks (OpenFrames' CoordinateAxes): lines from the origin along x, y and z, major and minor tick marks at their spacings, in the frame's color. Tick marks shrink beyond the axis length from the camera (OpenFrames' tick attenuation). The frame's own axes (arrows) are moved to the lines' ends when the length is set (as OpenFrames'); move them after that with move_x_axis, ..., or, for axes on a body, hide them (show_axes(NO_AXES)) and show the body's, which start at its surface.
