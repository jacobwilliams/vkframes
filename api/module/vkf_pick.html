ray

Picking geometry: what is under a point of a window (WindowProxy%pick).

A PickCamera is the camera and the cursor of a pick, in the scene's root frame: the eye and its axes (right, up, forward), the projection, the viewport's size and the cursor's position (-1 to 1, y up, as the window's events). Frames test their contents against it (ReferenceFrame%pick and its extensions): * points and line segments on the screen, within a tolerance in pixels (point_pick, segment_pick: the closest point of a segment, its fraction along the segment in the scene, perspective-correct, and its depth), for trajectories, markers and frames' origins; * a ray from the eye through the cursor against an ellipsoid (ray, ray_ellipsoid), for bodies; * the ray against a plane, a sphere and a line (ray_plane, ray_sphere, ray_line), for dragging (WindowProxy%cursor_ray, and vkf_handles' constraints); * bounding spheres on the screen (sphere_near), to skip whole chunks of a long trajectory. Depths are distances along the view direction (perspective) or from the eye's plane (orthographic); points closer than near are behind the eye.
