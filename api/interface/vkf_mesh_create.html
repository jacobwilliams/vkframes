1 for an unlit transparent surface (no textures), 2 for view lighting (frames' axes), else lit by the scene's lights with a material (vkf_mesh_material); plus 4 for vertices that change after the mesh is compiled (rewritten in place, then vkf_mesh_vertices_changed); plus 8 for a lit translucent surface (depth sorted: vkf_mesh_set_bound); plus 16 two-sided (lit: no faces culled); plus 32 a color per vertex (vkf_mesh_array 5; rgba each one's initial color)
