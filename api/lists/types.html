CoordinateAxes vkf_shapes ReferenceFrame

Coordinate axes with tick marks (OpenFrames' CoordinateAxes): lines from the origin along x, y and z, major and minor tick marks at their spacings, in the frame's color. Tick marks shrink beyond the axis length from the camera (OpenFrames' tick attenuation). The frame's own axes (arrows) are moved to the lines' ends when the length is set (as OpenFrames'); move them after that with move_x_axis, ..., or, for axes on a body, hide them (show_axes(NO_AXES)) and show the body's, which start at its surface.

MarkerArtist vkf_artists TrajectoryArtist

Draws markers at a trajectory's points (OpenFrames' MarkerArtist): at its start and end, and intermediate markers spaced by time, arc length or data points, counted from the start or from the end. Markers keep their size on screen (window units), or with auto-attenuation shrink when the camera is farther than the trajectory's size. In trace mode (an addition), the markers passed are drawn: the end marker (at the trajectory's end) only once the time reaches the end. Point colors (an addition, off by default: set_point_colors): each marker in the trajectory's color there (see set_point_colors).

RibbonArtist vkf_artists ChainArtist

Draws a translucent surface swept between two points at each point of a trajectory (not in OpenFrames): from the point given by the start data sources to the one given by the end data sources, with a quad between each point's pair and the next's. Default: from the frame's origin to the positions, sweeping out an orbit's plane (a fan, as the radius vectors of a SegmentArtist would fill it); other sources give e.g. a curtain from the positions down to a ground track stored as an optional. Unlit, seen from both sides, depth tested but writing no depth (as the equatorial plane of a RadialPlane), drawn to double precision as the curves (its edge on a CurveArtist's line through the same points).
