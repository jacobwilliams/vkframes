vkf_line_chain vkf_line_chain.f90

A growing set of points drawn from a chain of GPU buffers ("chunks"), for the trajectory artists: a line strip, separate segments (pairs of points), markers (one per point) or a ribbon (a translucent surface through pairs of points: a quad between each pair and the next). Points are written in place, as (high, low) float pairs for double precision (see src/vsg/vkf_layer.cpp: EyeRelative), with a strip's distance along it (for dash patterns, see shaders/line.vert; measured from the trajectory's points), and only the chunks written to are uploaded again. A full chain gets new chunks, each as large as the chain so far or as what is needed at once, but at least min_chunk and at most max_chunk points, so nothing is ever copied or reallocated, and changing a point uploads at most max_chunk points again. In a strip, consecutive chunks share their end point so that the line is continuous; in a ribbon, their end pair.
